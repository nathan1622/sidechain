About the logo

    
Once sidechain.global/brand/sidechain-icon.png is live, this page fills that URL in by itself and your signature points at it — the stable setup. If it cannot reach it, the logo travels along as an embedded image instead; Gmail usually accepts that, but it sometimes strips embedded images and leaves you with text only. You can also paste the URL yourself.

    
Viewing this page inside a Claude artifact, an external image stays blank here — the preview frame is not allowed to load external files. The copied signature still carries the correct URL.
